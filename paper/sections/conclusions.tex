\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

We measured how much routine preparation choices change redocking success on the
PoseBusters Benchmark set, one choice at a time and complex by complex, for four engines.
Preparation moved success by up to 10.5 percentage points within an engine, less than the
16.4 points that separated Gnina from Vina \result{and the rDock comparison}, and only the
size of the search box did so beyond chance; a tight box raised success for every engine. The
choices also reshuffled which complexes succeeded far more than seed-to-seed variation did,
even where the net rate barely moved. Docking comparisons should therefore state the search
space exactly, repeat runs over seeds and test differences complex by complex. The open
benchmark lets any new preparation step or engine be measured against the same baseline,
seed noise floor and paired statistics.
